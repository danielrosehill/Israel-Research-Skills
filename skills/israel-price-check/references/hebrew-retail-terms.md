# Hebrew retail terms

Israeli retailer and Zap search works best as
`<Hebrew category noun> <brand in Latin> <model in Latin>`. Translate only the
category noun.

## Commercial-intent words

- `מחיר <term> <brand>` ("price"): a strong shopping signal when you have a
  category but no model.
- `<term> בארץ` / `בישראל`: when international results drown the local ones.
- `יבואן רשמי` (official importer) vs `יבוא מקביל` (parallel import): use these
  to tell warranty-backed stock from grey-market stock.
- `אחריות` (warranty), `במלאי` (in stock), `אזל מהמלאי` (out of stock),
  `משלוח חינם` (free delivery), `איסוף עצמי` (self pickup),
  `לא כולל מע"מ` (excluding VAT).

## When you do not know the noun

Find the Hebrew term from a real listing:

1. Search `"<brand model>" site:il` for a known product in the class.
2. Read the noun from the retailer page's URL slug, `<title>` or breadcrumb.
   Skip news sites: ynet, walla, haaretz, calcalist, themarker, geektime.
3. Use the singular. Keep variants. Retailers disagree; chargers alone have
   three names.

Never invent a Hebrew term, and do not trust a dictionary translation alone for
retail jargon.
