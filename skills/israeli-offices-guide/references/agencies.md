# Israeli agencies — names and what they handle

People ask for an office by its colloquial name. Its page and letterhead use
the formal name. Search with one, and expect to read the other.

| Colloquial (Hebrew) | Formal (Hebrew) | English | Handles | Site |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| משרד הפנים | רשות האוכלוסין וההגירה | Population and Immigration Authority | Teudat zehut and ספח, passports, address change, civil status, visas | `gov.il` (blocks fetches); appointments via MyVisit |
| משרד הקליטה | משרד העלייה והקליטה | Ministry of Aliyah and Integration | Absorption basket, olim benefits, ulpan referrals, olim advisers | `gov.il` |
| ביטוח לאומי | המוסד לביטוח לאומי | National Insurance Institute | Child allowance, unemployment, maternity, disability, old age, income support, health-insurance registration, reserve-duty compensation | `btl.gov.il` |
| מס הכנסה | רשות המסים | Israel Tax Authority | Income tax, refunds, tax credit points, VAT, customs, purchase tax | `taxes.gov.il`, which now redirects to `gov.il/he/departments/israel_tax_authority` |
| קופת חולים | — | Health fund: Clalit, Maccabi, Meuhedet, Leumit | Doctors, clinics, medicines, supplementary insurance | each fund's site |
| לשכת התעסוקה | שירות התעסוקה | Israeli Employment Service | Registration required for unemployment benefit and some other benefits | `taasuka.gov.il` |
| עירייה | — | Municipality | Arnona and its discounts, parking, schools registration, local services, call centre 106 (varies) | the city's own site |
| חברת החשמל | חברת החשמל לישראל | Israel Electric Corporation | Electricity accounts, outages (103) | `iec.co.il` |
| רשות האכיפה והגבייה / הוצאה לפועל | — | Enforcement and Collection Authority | Debt enforcement files | `gov.il` |
| משרד התחבורה / משרד הרישוי | — | Ministry of Transport / Licensing Office | Driving licence conversion, vehicle licensing | `gov.il` |
| דואר ישראל | — | Israel Post | Mail, parcels, some government payments | `israelpost.co.il` |
| רשות הטבע והגנים | — | Nature and Parks Authority | National parks, nature reserves | `parks.org.il` |

## Reading an office page

| Hebrew | Meaning |
|---|---|
| קבלת קהל | Counter / walk-in reception |
| מוקד טלפוני / מענה טלפוני | Phone line |
| מענה אנושי | A human answering, as opposed to the automated menu |
| שירות עצמי / שירות מקוון | Online self-service |
| זימון תור / בתיאום מראש | By appointment |
| ללא תור | Walk-in |
| שפות שירות | Languages of service. This usually means "someone at the counter speaks it", not "always available" and not on the phone. |
| מה להביא / מסמכים נדרשים | What to bring / required documents |
| כתובת / כניסה / קומה | Address / entrance / floor |

Street names transliterate inconsistently, so keep the Hebrew address. That is
what Waze and taxi drivers accept.
